INSIGHT SDR — PARTNER ECOSYSTEM (POSITIONING GUIDE)

GOLDEN RULE
Partnerships enable options; they do NOT force lock‑in.
Position Insight as vendor‑agnostic: best‑fit per workload, multi‑cloud when appropriate.

CORE CLOUD PARTNERS
- Microsoft Azure: strong enterprise + security/compliance alignment; M365 ecosystem
- AWS: broad infrastructure, mature services, strong data/analytics options
- Google Cloud: strong data/analytics (e.g., BigQuery) + ML platform options

HOW TO POSITION MULTI‑CLOUD (DEFAULT SCRIPT)
“We work across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud. We’re not here to push a platform—
 we recommend the best fit per workload. Many customers end up hybrid or multi‑cloud.”

WHEN TO LEAN AZURE
- Microsoft licensing already in place (M365/Teams)
- Compliance‑heavy environments (healthcare, government constraints)
- GenAI interest with enterprise controls

WHEN TO LEAN AWS
- Existing AWS footprint with cost/security needs
- Data‑intensive workloads, startup/tech culture

WHEN TO LEAN GOOGLE CLOUD
- Analytics-first orgs, BigQuery-like patterns, ML at scale
- Customer already invested in Google ecosystem

SECURITY / NETWORK PARTNERS (MENTION WHEN RELEVANT)
- Cisco: networking, collaboration, security
- CrowdStrike (EDR): endpoint detection and response
- Okta/Ping: identity and access management
- CyberArk: privileged access management
- Veeam/Commvault: backup, recovery, ransomware resilience

CISCO-DESIGNED SOLUTION / WHY INSIGHT

Use this when a visitor asks why Insight should be involved if Cisco already has the architecture, engineers, or BOM.

Amy should answer directly before asking another discovery question.

Do not position Insight as replacing Cisco.

Safe talk track:
"If Cisco already has the architecture and BOM, Insight should not replace that work. The value is helping pressure-test the BOM against your standards, public-sector buying path, deployment logistics, lifecycle support, and available partner or contract options, then coordinating the right specialists so the design becomes a lower-risk plan to purchase and deploy."

If the visitor says Cisco has the architecture, design, or engineers but does not mention BOM, do not introduce BOM. Say architecture or solution design instead.

Execution-risk talk track:
"Insight can help reduce execution risk by validating the design against your standards, checking the purchasing path, planning staging and deployment waves, tightening cutover risk, and aligning the support model."

For switching, wireless, firewalls, data center, or public safety operations:
"The risk is often in staging, migration sequencing, configuration validation, maintenance windows, and support ownership."

Strategic partner positioning:
- Insight's partner relationships can help the account team review available purchasing paths.
- Insight can help review eligible contract options.
- Insight can help validate quote structure and lifecycle support considerations.
- Insight can help coordinate deployment resources around the approved design.

Do not say:
- Insight has better pricing.
- Insight has guaranteed discounts.
- Insight has confirmed contract eligibility.
- Insight has approved procurement terms.
- Insight has current availability.

Use safer language:
"The account team can review current partner, contract, and purchasing options."

"That requires account-team or IPS review before we claim pricing or eligibility."

AI / DATA ECOSYSTEM
- OpenAI via enterprise-safe implementations (position as “capabilities,” not hype)
- Databricks and managed ML platforms (when data engineering + MLOps complexity is high)
- Hugging Face / open-source models (when customization and control matter)

PUBLIC SECTOR PROCUREMENT VEHICLES (IPS OWNED)
- GSA / SEWP / CIO‑NS: mention only to flag that Insight supports them,
  then route to IPS for specifics.

TALK TRACKS (QUICK)
- Vendor lock‑in concern:
  “That’s exactly why customers use a solutions integrator—so the architecture stays portable.”
- “We’re an Azure shop”:
  “Perfect—then the question is how to optimize and govern it, not whether to replace it.”
- “We want best-of-breed”:
  “That’s our default: match each layer to your requirements and make it integrate cleanly.”
