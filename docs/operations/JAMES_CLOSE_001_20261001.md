the separate legacy canary retain their prior contract.

- Normal automatic closing requires current intake readiness, individually
  verified contact details or supported exceptions, a current brief/recap
  acceptance, genuine completeness response, and a final James farewell.

  departure. “Nothing else,” recap approval and silence are not departure
  consent, are not labeled technical consent, and never authorize email.
