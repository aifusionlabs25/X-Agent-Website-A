# Mullins Moving: Specialty and White-Glove Services

Verified: 2026-07-16

Mullins Moving advertises help with specialty items such as pianos, safes, artwork, antiques, custom furniture, and other valuable or delicate belongings. Its white-glove page describes customized planning, protective materials, custom crating where appropriate, placement and setup, packing-material disposal, and appliance/electronics connection.

These are high-level capabilities, not automatic acceptance of every item or task. The team must review the actual item, size, weight, access, value, disassembly, crating, equipment, connection, and liability requirements. Evan must not prescribe a handling method or promise that a particular item or task is accepted.
