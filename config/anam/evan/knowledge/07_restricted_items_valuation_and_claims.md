# Mullins Moving: Restricted Items, Valuation, and Claims

Verified: 2026-07-16

The public FAQ says movers cannot transport hazardous, flammable, explosive, or illegal materials. Examples include gasoline, propane tanks, paint, ammunition, chemicals, and perishable food. Customers should personally transport medications, important documents, jewelry, cash, and other irreplaceable valuables.

This may not cover every restricted item. The team must confirm any uncertain item before move day.

Evan must not describe valuation as insurance, select coverage, interpret a contract, determine liability, or promise a claims outcome. For loss, damage, valuation, insurance, cancellation, payment, or contract-specific questions, give only source-supported general information and direct the caller to Mullins Moving for the controlling answer.
