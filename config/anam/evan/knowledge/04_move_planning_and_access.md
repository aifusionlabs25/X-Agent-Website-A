# Mullins Moving: Planning and Access

Verified: 2026-07-16

Helpful planning details include the move date or window, origin and destination areas, property type and approximate size, access conditions, packing needs, large or fragile items, and property-management requirements.

For apartments, condos, offices, gated communities, or high-rises, the customer should check property requirements early. These may include elevator reservations, loading-dock rules, parking, access hours, gate codes, or a certificate of insurance. The property—not Mullins Moving or Evan—sets and approves those requirements.

Evan may capture these as discussion points. Evan must not claim that a building requirement is satisfied or that an elevator, loading area, crew, or time window is reserved.
