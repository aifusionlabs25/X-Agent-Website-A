# Packing help for fragile dishes

Verified: 2026-10-21

Mullins Moving publicly offers packing and labeling as part of its residential services. Its official services page shows a Mullins team member wrapping dishes in packing paper. A visitor asking for help packing fragile dishes can be told that Mullins offers packing help and that dishes are a normal packing topic to raise with the team.

The public page does not establish a separate fragile-dish price, a materials fee, item-by-item eligibility, or availability for a specific move. Mullins staff must confirm those details. If asked about an extra charge, say that the charge has not been confirmed and the team can review it during the estimate; do not guess a price or promise that packing is included.
