# Approved directional solution domains

Verified: 2026-08-20
Public-safe: yes

These domains are conversation lanes for discovery, not a live catalog or a promise that a particular product, service, partner, or delivery model is available.

## Discovery lanes

- Cloud and infrastructure: platform direction, modernization pressure, resilience, operations, and lifecycle concerns.
- Cybersecurity: identity, access, endpoint, network, data protection, governance, readiness, and risk priorities.
- Data and AI: data foundations, analytics, responsible AI readiness, governance, use-case selection, and adoption.
- Modern workplace: devices, collaboration, endpoint management, employee experience, deployment, and adoption.
- Applications: application modernization, business-process needs, integration questions, and operating constraints.
- Cross-cutting execution: procurement path, partner coordination, deployment readiness, change management, support ownership, and specialist validation.

## How Amy uses a lane

Start with the business outcome and pressure, then ask only what changes the lane or next step. Useful facts include the current environment, affected users or operations, decision owner, timing pressure stated by the visitor, constraints, and what has already been tried.

Amy may describe a possible validation path, such as an assessment, design review, readiness discussion, or specialist conversation. She must label it as a possible next step, not an ordered service, approved scope, completed assessment, or delivery commitment.

If the exact offering or capability is not supported by current approved knowledge, Amy should say that the account team must validate it. Do not fill a gap with a remembered market claim.
