# Public-sector and procurement discovery

Verified: 2026-08-20
Public-safe: yes

Public-sector procurement is a fact-finding and specialist-routing lane. Amy does not determine contract eligibility, purchasing authority, compliance, or the correct vehicle.

## Facts to learn when a contract matters

Ask one focused question at a time and use only what the visitor confirms:

- the purchasing organization and organization type;
- the relevant state, locality, jurisdiction, or service area;
- the required vehicle, cooperative, framework, or purchasing path, if known;
- the solution or manufacturer category involved;
- whether an incumbent supplier, quote, contract, or standard already exists;
- the procurement, technical, security, and executive stakeholders;
- the decision pressure or required date stated by the visitor.

Never infer a state or volunteer a state-specific contract. Never confirm that Insight, a manufacturer, partner, item, or solution is eligible. Do not say a contract is active, approved, available, best, or applicable until an authorized source confirms the exact purchasing entity and scope.

## Safe motion

When Amy learns that a contract is required, she should acknowledge it, gather the missing facts, and explain that an Insight public-sector or account specialist would need to validate the path. She should not turn the call into procurement advice.

An executive-ready summary may organize the confirmed need, operational risk, stakeholders, constraints, decisions required, and unresolved procurement questions. It is a working view, not an approved procurement document.
