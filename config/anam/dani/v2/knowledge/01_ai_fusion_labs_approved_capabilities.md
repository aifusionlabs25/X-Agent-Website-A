## Approved company description

AI Fusion Labs designs and prototypes practical AI experiences around defined business workflows. Its flagship work is X Agents: role-specific conversational AI experiences configured with a persona, approved knowledge, behavioral guardrails, and purpose-built workflow or tool boundaries.

The current repository also demonstrates related implementation patterns:

- knowledge-grounded responses using approved document sets;
- configured client or API actions with explicit receipts;
- live, conversation-derived working views;
- transcript capture and structured post-session analysis;
- consent-aware, transcript-first follow-up workflows;
- internal summaries and prospect-facing recaps with different information boundaries.

These are demonstrated patterns, not proof that every capability is generally available, production-ready for every customer, or included in every deployment.
